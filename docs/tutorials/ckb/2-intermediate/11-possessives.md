# 11. My, your, his: the personal clitics

**Level:** 🟡 Intermediate · lesson 11 of 23

**On this page:** [Six small endings that do a lot](#six-small-endings-that-do-a-lot) · [On a noun](#on-a-noun) · [At the end of the phrase](#at-the-end-of-the-phrase) · [To have](#to-have) · [With prepositions](#with-prepositions) · [Practice](#practice) · [Summary](#summary)

## Six small endings that do a lot

Sorani has a second set of personal markers, the **clitics** (also called *set 2*). They attach to the end of a word or phrase:

| person | clitic | Latin | as possessive |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1sg | **-م** | -(i)m | my |
| 2sg | **-ت** | -(i)t | your |
| 3sg | **-ی** | -î / -y | his, her, its |
| 1pl | **-مان** | -man | our |
| 2pl | **-تان** | -tan | your (pl) |
| 3pl | **-یان** | -yan | their |

They are the most important endings in Sorani. Here they mean *my, your, …*; in lesson 14 the same clitics mark **who did it** in the past tense, and in lesson 22 they mark objects.

## On a noun

Attach the clitic either to the bare noun or, for a specific thing (*my book* = the one we know about), to the definite noun:

| person | pronoun | my/your… + noun | the noun + clitic |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1sg | من | **کتێبم**<br><sub>kitêbim</sub> | **کتێبەکەم**<br><sub>kitêbekem</sub> |
| 2sg | تۆ | **کتێبت**<br><sub>kitêbit</sub> | **کتێبەکەت**<br><sub>kitêbeket</sub> |
| 3sg | ئەو | **کتێبی**<br><sub>kitêbî</sub> | **کتێبەکەی**<br><sub>kitêbekey</sub> |
| 1pl | ئێمە | **کتێبمان**<br><sub>kitêbman</sub> | **کتێبەکەمان**<br><sub>kitêbekeman</sub> |
| 2pl | ئێوە | **کتێبتان**<br><sub>kitêbtan</sub> | **کتێبەکەتان**<br><sub>kitêbeketan</sub> |
| 3pl | ئەوان | **کتێبیان**<br><sub>kitêbyan</sub> | **کتێبەکەیان**<br><sub>kitêbekeyan</sub> |

For the plural, attach to the definite plural: کتێبەکانم *my books*, ماڵەکانیان *their houses*.

## At the end of the phrase

The clitic goes at the **end of the whole noun phrase**, after any adjective:

| phrase | meaning |
|---|---|
| کتێبە نوێیەکە**م** | my new book |
| ماڵە گەورەکە**یان** | their big house |
| باوکی هاوڕێکە**م** | my friend's father (the father of my friend) |

For emphasis, use the ezafe and a pronoun instead: کتێبی **من** *MY book* (not yours).

## To have

*To have* is expressed as *X-of-mine exists*: the clitic on the possessed thing + **هەیە** (negative **نییە**):

<!-- gen:sentences ipa=1
کتێبێکم هەیە. | I have a book.
ئۆتۆمبێلت هەیە؟ | Do you have a car?
براکەی دوو کچی هەیە. | His brother has two daughters.
پارەمان نییە. | We don't have money.
کاتتان هەیە؟ | Do you (pl) have time?
-->
| # | Kurdish | Latin | IPA | English |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | کتێبێکم هەیە. | kitêbêkim heye. | /k(ɯ)teːbeːk(ɯ)m hæjæ/ | I have a book. |
| 2 | ئۆتۆمبێلت هەیە؟ | otombêlt heye? | /ʔoːtoːmbeːlt hæjæ/ | Do you have a car? |
| 3 | براکەی دوو کچی هەیە. | birakey dû kiçî heye. | /b(ɯ)ɾaːkæj duː k(ɯ)tʃiː hæjæ/ | His brother has two daughters. |
| 4 | پارەمان نییە. | pareman nîye. | /paːɾæmaːn niːjæ/ | We don't have money. |

## With prepositions

After **لە** and **بە** the clitic attaches to a special form of the preposition: لە → **لێ**, بە → **پێ**:

| form | meaning |
|---|---|
| لێم | from me |
| لێی | from him / her |
| پێت | to / with you |
| پێیان | to / with them |

پێم بڵێ *Tell me* (lit. to-me say).

## Practice

1. *my house* (specific) and *our house*.
2. *their books*.
3. *I don't have a car.*

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. ماڵەکەم — ماڵەکەمان
2. کتێبەکانیان
3. ئۆتۆمبێلم نییە.

</details>

## Summary

* Clitics **م ت ی مان تان یان** = my, your, his/her, our, your, their.
* They go at the end of the noun phrase: کتێبە نوێیەکەم.
* *To have* = clitic on the thing + هەیە.
