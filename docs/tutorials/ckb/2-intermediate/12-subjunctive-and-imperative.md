# 12. Wishes, possibilities and commands

**Level:** 🟡 Intermediate · lesson 12 of 23

**On this page:** [The subjunctive](#the-subjunctive) · [When to use it](#when-to-use-it) · [“I want”: ویستن](#i-want-ویستن) · [“I can”: توانین](#i-can-توانین) · [The imperative](#the-imperative) · [Practice](#practice) · [Summary](#summary)

## The subjunctive

The **subjunctive** is the verb form for things that are wanted, possible, uncertain or not yet real: *that I go*, *(so) that I may go*, *if I go*. It looks like the present with **ب** instead of **دە**:

> **ب** + present stem + set-1 ending (م یت ێت ین ن ن); negative **نە**

<!-- gen:verb verb=کردن cells=present,subj,subj_neg -->
*کردن* (kirdin) “to do, to make” — present stem **کە**, past stem **کرد**, transitive

| person | pronoun | Present | Subjunctive | Subjunctive negative |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1sg | من | **دەکەم**<br><sub>dekem</sub> | **بکەم**<br><sub>bikem</sub> | **نەکەم**<br><sub>nekem</sub> |
| 2sg | تۆ | **دەکەیت**<br><sub>dekeyt</sub> | **بکەیت**<br><sub>bikeyt</sub> | **نەکەیت**<br><sub>nekeyt</sub> |
| 3sg | ئەو | **دەکات**<br><sub>dekat</sub> | **بکات**<br><sub>bikat</sub> | **نەکات**<br><sub>nekat</sub> |
| 1pl | ئێمە | **دەکەین**<br><sub>dekeyn</sub> | **بکەین**<br><sub>bikeyn</sub> | **نەکەین**<br><sub>nekeyn</sub> |
| 2pl | ئێوە | **دەکەن**<br><sub>deken</sub> | **بکەن**<br><sub>biken</sub> | **نەکەن**<br><sub>neken</sub> |
| 3pl | ئەوان | **دەکەن**<br><sub>deken</sub> | **بکەن**<br><sub>biken</sub> | **نەکەن**<br><sub>neken</sub> |
<!-- /gen -->

<!-- gen:verb verb=چوون cells=subj,subj_neg -->
*چوون* (çûn) “to go” — present stem **چ**, past stem **چوو**, intransitive

| person | pronoun | Subjunctive | Subjunctive negative |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1sg | من | **بچم**<br><sub>biçim</sub> | **نەچم**<br><sub>neçim</sub> |
| 2sg | تۆ | **بچیت**<br><sub>biçît</sub> | **نەچیت**<br><sub>neçît</sub> |
| 3sg | ئەو | **بچێت**<br><sub>biçêt</sub> | **نەچێت**<br><sub>neçêt</sub> |
| 1pl | ئێمە | **بچین**<br><sub>biçîn</sub> | **نەچین**<br><sub>neçîn</sub> |
| 2pl | ئێوە | **بچن**<br><sub>biçin</sub> | **نەچن**<br><sub>neçin</sub> |
| 3pl | ئەوان | **بچن**<br><sub>biçin</sub> | **نەچن**<br><sub>neçin</sub> |
<!-- /gen -->

## When to use it

| after | meaning | example |
|---|---|---|
| **دەمەوێت** | I want (to) | دەمەوێت بچم. *I want to go.* |
| **دەتوانم** | I can | دەتوانم بێم. *I can come.* |
| **با** | let's / let | با بڕۆین! *Let's go!* |
| **پێویستە** | it is necessary, must | پێویستە بخوێنیت. *You must study.* |
| **ئەگەر** | if | ئەگەر بێیت، دەچین. *If you come, we'll go.* |
| **بۆ ئەوەی** | in order to | هاتم بۆ ئەوەی ببینمت. *I came to see you.* |

## “I want”: ویستن

ویستن *to want* is special: in the present, the person is shown by a **clitic** (lesson 11), as if saying *to me it is wanted*:

<!-- gen:verb verb=ویستن cells=present,present_neg -->
*ویستن* (wîstin) “to want” — present stem **وێ**, past stem **ویست**, transitive

| person | pronoun | Present | Present negative |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1sg | من | **دەمەوێت**<br><sub>demewêt</sub> | **نامەوێت**<br><sub>namewêt</sub> |
| 2sg | تۆ | **دەتەوێت**<br><sub>detewêt</sub> | **ناتەوێت**<br><sub>natewêt</sub> |
| 3sg | ئەو | **دەیەوێت**<br><sub>deyewêt</sub> | **نایەوێت**<br><sub>nayewêt</sub> |
| 1pl | ئێمە | **دەمانەوێت**<br><sub>demanewêt</sub> | **نامانەوێت**<br><sub>namanewêt</sub> |
| 2pl | ئێوە | **دەتانەوێت**<br><sub>detanewêt</sub> | **ناتانەوێت**<br><sub>natanewêt</sub> |
| 3pl | ئەوان | **دەیانەوێت**<br><sub>deyanewêt</sub> | **نایانەوێت**<br><sub>nayanewêt</sub> |

## The imperative

Commands are formed on the present stem with **ب**; the singular adds **-ە** after a consonant, the plural adds **-ن**. The **prohibitive** (*don't!*) uses **مە**:

<!-- gen:verb verb=کردن cells=imperative,prohibitive -->
*کردن* (kirdin) “to do, to make” — present stem **کە**, past stem **کرد**, transitive

| person | pronoun | Imperative | Prohibitive |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2sg | تۆ | **بکە**<br><sub>bike</sub> | **مەکە**<br><sub>meke</sub> |
| 2pl | ئێوە | **بکەن**<br><sub>biken</sub> | **مەکەن**<br><sub>meken</sub> |
<!-- /gen -->

<!-- gen:verb verb=نووسین cells=imperative,prohibitive -->
*نووسین* (nûsîn) “to write” — present stem **نووس**, past stem **نووسی**, transitive

| person | pronoun | Imperative | Prohibitive |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2sg | تۆ | **بنووسە**<br><sub>binûse</sub> | **مەنووسە**<br><sub>menûse</sub> |
| 2pl | ئێوە | **بنووسن**<br><sub>binûsin</sub> | **مەنووسن**<br><sub>menûsin</sub> |
<!-- /gen -->

<!-- gen:verb verb=خواردن cells=imperative,prohibitive -->
*خواردن* (xwardin) “to eat” — present stem **خۆ**, past stem **خوارد**, transitive

| person | pronoun | Imperative | Prohibitive |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2sg | تۆ | **بخۆ**<br><sub>bixo</sub> | **مەخۆ**<br><sub>mexo</sub> |
| 2pl | ئێوە | **بخۆن**<br><sub>bixon</sub> | **مەخۆن**<br><sub>mexon</sub> |
<!-- /gen -->

Two common verbs have irregular commands:

<!-- gen:verb verb=هاتن cells=imperative,prohibitive -->
*هاتن* (hatin) “to come” — present stem **ێ**, past stem **هات**, intransitive

| person | pronoun | Imperative | Prohibitive |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2sg | تۆ | **وەرە**<br><sub>were</sub> | **مەیە**<br><sub>meye</sub> |
| 2pl | ئێوە | **وەرن**<br><sub>wern</sub> | **مەیەن**<br><sub>meyen</sub> |
<!-- /gen -->

<!-- gen:verb verb=چوون cells=imperative,prohibitive -->
*چوون* (çûn) “to go” — present stem **چ**, past stem **چوو**, intransitive

| person | pronoun | Imperative | Prohibitive |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2sg | تۆ | **بڕۆ**<br><sub>birro</sub> | **مەچە**<br><sub>meçe</sub> |
| 2pl | ئێوە | **بڕۆن**<br><sub>birron</sub> | **مەچن**<br><sub>meçin</sub> |
<!-- /gen -->

بڕۆ *go!* is borrowed from ڕۆیشتن.

## Practice

1. *I want to eat.*
2. *Write!* (to one person) and *Don't write!* (to several).
3. *We can't go.*

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. دەمەوێت بخۆم.
2. بنووسە! — مەنووسن!
3. ناتوانین بچین.

</details>

## Summary

* Subjunctive = **ب** + present stem + ending (negative **نە**), used after *want, can, must, let's, if, in order to*.
* ویستن: دەمەوێت، دەتەوێت، دەیەوێت…
* Imperative **ب…ە / ب…ن**, prohibitive **مە…**; وەرە *come!*, بڕۆ *go!*
