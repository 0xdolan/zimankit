# 15. Was doing, used to do: the past progressive

**Level:** 🟡 Intermediate · lesson 15 of 23

**On this page:** [Form](#form) · [Meaning](#meaning) · [All past forms side by side](#all-past-forms-side-by-side) · [Practice](#practice) · [Summary](#summary)

## Form

Put **دە** in front of the past stem:

* **intransitive**: دە + past stem + set-1 ending: **دەهاتم** *I was coming / used to come*;
* **transitive**: the agent clitic goes **between** دە and the stem (or on an earlier object): **دەمکرد** *I was doing / used to do*.

The negative is **نەدە-**, with the clitic after نە for transitive verbs: **نەدەهاتم**, **نەمدەکرد**.

## Meaning

The past progressive is used for

1. an action **in progress** in the past: *I was reading when he came*;
2. a **habit** in the past: *I used to read every evening*;
3. the result of an unreal condition: *I would go (if …)*.

## Practice

1. *I was going.*
2. *She used to write letters* (نامە).
3. *We weren't eating.*

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. دەچووم
2. نامەی دەنووسی
3. نەماندەخوارد

</details>

## Summary

* Past progressive = **دە** + past stem; transitive: clitic after دە (دەمکرد) or on the object.
* Negative **نەدە-** (نەمدەکرد).
* Used for ongoing and habitual past actions and for *would*.
