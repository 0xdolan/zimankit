# 13. The past tense I: intransitive verbs

**Level:** 🟡 Intermediate · lesson 13 of 23

**On this page:** [Transitive and intransitive](#transitive-and-intransitive) · [The simple past](#the-simple-past) · [In sentences](#in-sentences) · [Practice](#practice) · [Summary](#summary)

## Transitive and intransitive

From now on it matters whether a verb is **transitive** (it can take a direct object: *to eat something*, *to see someone*) or **intransitive** (it cannot: *to come*, *to go*, *to sleep*). Sorani builds the past of the two kinds **differently**. This lesson covers intransitive verbs, which work just like English; lesson 14 covers transitive ones.

Every verb page in the [reference](../reference/verbs/README.md) says which kind a verb is.

## The simple past

> past stem + set-1 ending (م یت — ین ن ن)

The past stem is the infinitive without **ن**. The 3sg has **no ending**:

<!-- gen:verb verb=هاتن cells=past,past_neg -->
*هاتن* (hatin) “to come” — present stem **ێ**, past stem **هات**, intransitive

| person | pronoun | Simple past | Past negative |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1sg | من | **هاتم**<br><sub>hatim</sub> | **نەهاتم**<br><sub>nehatim</sub> |
| 2sg | تۆ | **هاتیت**<br><sub>hatît</sub> | **نەهاتیت**<br><sub>nehatît</sub> |
| 3sg | ئەو | **هات**<br><sub>hat</sub> | **نەهات**<br><sub>nehat</sub> |
| 1pl | ئێمە | **هاتین**<br><sub>hatîn</sub> | **نەهاتین**<br><sub>nehatîn</sub> |
| 2pl | ئێوە | **هاتن**<br><sub>hatin</sub> | **نەهاتن**<br><sub>nehatin</sub> |
| 3pl | ئەوان | **هاتن**<br><sub>hatin</sub> | **نەهاتن**<br><sub>nehatin</sub> |
<!-- /gen -->

The negative adds **نە**: هاتم *I came* → نەهاتم *I didn't come*.

In speech the 2sg is often shortened to **-ی**: هاتی *you came*.

## In sentences

<!-- gen:sentences ipa=1
دوێنێ هاتم. | I came yesterday.
ئەوان چوون بۆ بازاڕ. | They went to the market.
منداڵەکە کەوت. | The child fell.
بۆچی نەهاتیت؟ | Why didn't you come?
ئێمە زوو نووستین. | We slept early.
-->
| # | Kurdish | Latin | IPA | English |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | دوێنێ هاتم. | dwênê hatim. | /dweːneː haːt(ɯ)m/ | I came yesterday. |
| 2 | ئەوان چوون بۆ بازاڕ. | ewan çûn bo bazarr. | /ʔæwaːn tʃuːn boː baːzaːr/ | They went to the market. |
| 3 | منداڵەکە کەوت. | minidalleke kewt. | /m(ɯ)n(ɯ)daːɫækæ kæwt/ | The child fell. |
| 4 | بۆچی نەهاتیت؟ | boçî nehatît? | /boːtʃiː næhaːtiːt/ | Why didn't you come? |
| 5 | ئێمە زوو نووستین. | ême zû nûstîn. | /ʔeːmæ zuː nuːstiːn/ | We slept early. |
<!-- /gen -->

Notice that the subject (من *I*, ئەوان *they*) is the same as in the present. The verb agrees with it, as in English. Hold on to that; in the next lesson it changes.

## Summary

* Intransitive past = past stem + **م یت — ین ن ن**; negative **نە**.
* The 3sg is the bare past stem: هات *he came*.
