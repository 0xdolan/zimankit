# 21. Irregular and special verbs

**Level:** 🔴 Advanced · lesson 21 of 23

**On this page:** [هاتن *to come*](#هاتن-to-come) · [چوون *to go*](#چوون-to-go) · [ویستن *to want*](#ویستن-to-want) · [بوون *to be, to become*](#بوون-to-be-to-become) · [Verbs with unpredictable present stems](#verbs-with-unpredictable-present-stems) · [Summary](#summary)

Sorani verbs are remarkably regular once you know the two stems. The handful of real irregularities are all in very common verbs, so learn them early.

## هاتن *to come*

The present stem is **ێ**, which fuses with the prefixes: **دێم** (not \*دەێم), negative **نایەم**, subjunctive **بێم**, negative subjunctive **نەیەم**, imperative **وەرە / وەرن**, prohibitive **مەیە / مەیەن**.

<!-- gen:verb verb=هاتن cells=present,present_neg,subj,subj_neg -->
*هاتن* (hatin) “to come” — present stem **ێ**, past stem **هات**, intransitive

| person | pronoun | Present | Present negative | Subjunctive | Subjunctive negative |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1sg | من | **دێم**<br><sub>dêm</sub> | **نایەم**<br><sub>nayem</sub> | **بێم**<br><sub>bêm</sub> | **نەیەم**<br><sub>neyem</sub> |
| 2sg | تۆ | **دێیت**<br><sub>dêyt</sub> | **نایەیت**<br><sub>nayeyt</sub> | **بێیت**<br><sub>bêyt</sub> | **نەیەیت**<br><sub>neyeyt</sub> |
| 3sg | ئەو | **دێت**<br><sub>dêt</sub> | **نایەت**<br><sub>nayet</sub> | **بێت**<br><sub>bêt</sub> | **نەیەت**<br><sub>neyet</sub> |
| 1pl | ئێمە | **دێین**<br><sub>dêyn</sub> | **نایەین**<br><sub>nayeyn</sub> | **بێین**<br><sub>bêyn</sub> | **نەیەین**<br><sub>neyeyn</sub> |
| 2pl | ئێوە | **دێن**<br><sub>dên</sub> | **نایەن**<br><sub>nayen</sub> | **بێن**<br><sub>bên</sub> | **نەیەن**<br><sub>neyen</sub> |
| 3pl | ئەوان | **دێن**<br><sub>dên</sub> | **نایەن**<br><sub>nayen</sub> | **بێن**<br><sub>bên</sub> | **نەیەن**<br><sub>neyen</sub> |
<!-- /gen -->

*هاتن* (hatin) “to come” — present stem **ێ**, past stem **هات**, intransitive

## چوون *to go*

Regular, except that the imperative is borrowed from ڕۆیشتن: **بڕۆ / بڕۆن** *go!*

<!-- gen:verb verb=چوون cells=present,past,imperative -->
*چوون* (çûn) “to go” — present stem **چ**, past stem **چوو**, intransitive

| person | pronoun | Present | Simple past | Imperative |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1sg | من | **دەچم**<br><sub>deçim</sub> | **چووم**<br><sub>çûm</sub> | — |
| 2sg | تۆ | **دەچیت**<br><sub>deçît</sub> | **چوویت**<br><sub>çûyt</sub> | **بڕۆ**<br><sub>birro</sub> |
| 3sg | ئەو | **دەچێت**<br><sub>deçêt</sub> | **چوو**<br><sub>çû</sub> | — |
| 1pl | ئێمە | **دەچین**<br><sub>deçîn</sub> | **چووین**<br><sub>çûyn</sub> | — |
| 2pl | ئێوە | **دەچن**<br><sub>deçin</sub> | **چوون**<br><sub>çûn</sub> | **بڕۆن**<br><sub>birron</sub> |
| 3pl | ئەوان | **دەچن**<br><sub>deçin</sub> | **چوون**<br><sub>çûn</sub> | — |
<!-- /gen -->

## ویستن *to want*

The present marks the person with a **clitic**, not an ending: دە**م**ەوێت *I want*. There is no imperative and no passive.

<!-- gen:verb verb=ویستن cells=present,present_neg,subj -->
*ویستن* (wîstin) “to want” — present stem **وێ**, past stem **ویست**, transitive

| person | pronoun | Present | Present negative | Subjunctive |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1sg | من | **دەمەوێت**<br><sub>demewêt</sub> | **نامەوێت**<br><sub>namewêt</sub> | **بمەوێت**<br><sub>bimewêt</sub> |
| 2sg | تۆ | **دەتەوێت**<br><sub>detewêt</sub> | **ناتەوێت**<br><sub>natewêt</sub> | **بتەوێت**<br><sub>bitewêt</sub> |
| 3sg | ئەو | **دەیەوێت**<br><sub>deyewêt</sub> | **نایەوێت**<br><sub>nayewêt</sub> | **بیەوێت**<br><sub>byewêt</sub> |
| 1pl | ئێمە | **دەمانەوێت**<br><sub>demanewêt</sub> | **نامانەوێت**<br><sub>namanewêt</sub> | **بمانەوێت**<br><sub>bimanewêt</sub> |
| 2pl | ئێوە | **دەتانەوێت**<br><sub>detanewêt</sub> | **ناتانەوێت**<br><sub>natanewêt</sub> | **بتانەوێت**<br><sub>bitanewêt</sub> |
| 3pl | ئەوان | **دەیانەوێت**<br><sub>deyanewêt</sub> | **نایانەوێت**<br><sub>nayanewêt</sub> | **بیانەوێت**<br><sub>byanewêt</sub> |
<!-- /gen -->

## بوون *to be, to become*

بوون is the verb *to become*, and the past and future of *to be*:

<!-- gen:verb verb=بوون cells=present,past,perfect,subj -->
*بوون* (bûn) “to be, to become” — present stem **ب**, past stem **بوو**, intransitive

| person | pronoun | Present | Simple past | Present perfect | Subjunctive |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1sg | من | **دەبم**<br><sub>debim</sub> | **بووم**<br><sub>bûm</sub> | **بووم**<br><sub>bûm</sub> | **ببم**<br><sub>bibim</sub> |
| 2sg | تۆ | **دەبیت**<br><sub>debît</sub> | **بوویت**<br><sub>bûyt</sub> | **بوویت**<br><sub>bûyt</sub> | **ببیت**<br><sub>bibît</sub> |
| 3sg | ئەو | **دەبێت**<br><sub>debêt</sub> | **بوو**<br><sub>bû</sub> | **بووە**<br><sub>bûwe</sub> | **ببێت**<br><sub>bibêt</sub> |
| 1pl | ئێمە | **دەبین**<br><sub>debîn</sub> | **بووین**<br><sub>bûyn</sub> | **بووین**<br><sub>bûyn</sub> | **ببین**<br><sub>bibîn</sub> |
| 2pl | ئێوە | **دەبن**<br><sub>debin</sub> | **بوون**<br><sub>bûn</sub> | **بوون**<br><sub>bûn</sub> | **ببن**<br><sub>bibin</sub> |
| 3pl | ئەوان | **دەبن**<br><sub>debin</sub> | **بوون**<br><sub>bûn</sub> | **بوون**<br><sub>bûn</sub> | **ببن**<br><sub>bibin</sub> |
<!-- /gen -->

* **دەبم** *I become, I will be*: مامۆستا دەبم *I will become a teacher*.
* **بووم** *I was, I became*: لە ماڵ بووم *I was at home*.
* *To have* in the past uses **هەبوو**: کتێبێکم هەبوو *I had a book*.

## Verbs with unpredictable present stems

These are not irregular in their endings, but their present stems cannot be guessed from the infinitive. They are the ones to memorise:

| infinitive | present stem | 1sg present |
|---|---|---|
| کردن *do* | کە | دەکەم |
| بردن *carry* | بە | دەبەم |
| خواردن *eat* | خۆ | دەخۆم |
| گوتن *say* | ڵێ | دەڵێم |
| بینین / دیتن *see* | بین | دەبینم |
| کوشتن *kill* | کوژ | دەکوژم |
| ناردن *send* | نێر | دەنێرم |
| شوشتن *wash* | شۆ | دەشۆم |
| هێشتن *let* | هێڵ | دەهێڵم |

The full list is on the [verb reference page](../reference/verbs/README.md).

## Summary

* هاتن: دێم / نایەم / وەرە / مەیە.
* چوون: imperative بڕۆ.
* ویستن: دەمەوێت (clitic subject).
* بوون: *become*, and the past of *be*.
