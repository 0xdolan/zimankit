# 18. The perfect tenses

**Level:** 🔴 Advanced · lesson 18 of 23

**On this page:** [Present perfect: *I have come, I have done*](#present-perfect-i-have-come-i-have-done) · [Past perfect: *I had come, I had done*](#past-perfect-i-had-come-i-had-done) · [With an object](#with-an-object) · [Meaning](#meaning) · [Summary](#summary)

## Present perfect: *I have come, I have done*

The present perfect is built on the **past participle**: the past stem + **وو** (after a consonant) or **و** (after ی / ا):

| infinitive | past stem | participle |
|---|---|---|
| هاتن | هات | هاتوو |
| کردن | کرد | کردوو |
| نووسین | نووسی | نووسیو |
| دان | دا | داو |

* **Intransitive**: participle + present copula endings: هاتوو**م** *I have come*, هاتوو**ە** *he has come*.
* **Transitive**: participle + the **agent clitic** + **-ە** (the copula *is*): کردوو**مە** *I have done* (lit. done-by-me-is), کردوو**یەتی** *he has done*.

<!-- gen:verb verb=هاتن cells=perfect,pluperfect show=translit,ipa -->

| 3pl | ئەوان | **کردوویانە**<br><sub>kiridûyane</sub><br><sub>/k(ɯ)ɾ(ɯ)duːjaːnæ/</sub> | **کردبوویان**<br><sub>kirdibûyan</sub><br><sub>/k(ɯ)ɾd(ɯ)buːjaːn/</sub> |

> Past tenses of a transitive verb are **ergative**: the person marker is the set-2 clitic (م ت ی مان تان یان) of the **agent**, not a verb ending.

## Past perfect: *I had come, I had done*

Past stem + **بوو** + ending (intransitive) or + **بوو** + clitic (transitive): هاتبووم *I had come*, کردبووم *I had done*.

## With an object

As in every past tense, the agent clitic moves to the object when there is one, and the verb keeps only the copula **-ە**:

<!-- gen:sentences ipa=1

هیچم نەکردووە. | I haven't done anything.
هێشتا نەهاتووە. | He hasn't come yet.
کاتێک گەیشتم، ئەوان ڕۆیشتبوون. | When I arrived, they had left.
نامەکەی نووسیبوو. | He had written the letter.
-->
| # | Kurdish | Latin | IPA | English |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ئەم کتێبەم خوێندووە. | em kitêbem xwêndûwe. | /ʔæm k(ɯ)teːbæm xweːnduːwæ/ | I have read this book. |
| 2 | هیچم نەکردووە. | hîçim nekridûwe. | /hiːtʃ(ɯ)m nækɾ(ɯ)duːwæ/ | I haven't done anything. |
| 3 | هێشتا نەهاتووە. | hêşta nehatûwe. | /heːʃtaː næhaːtuːwæ/ | He hasn't come yet. |

## Meaning

The present perfect is used much as in English, for past actions with present relevance. It is **also** the normal way to report something you did not witness yourself: *ئەو چووە بۆ ئەوروپا* “he has (apparently) gone to Europe”.

## Summary

* Present perfect: intransitive **هاتووم**, transitive **کردوومە** (clitic + ە).
* Past perfect: **هاتبووم**, **کردبووم**.
* The agent clitic still jumps to the object: کتێبەکەم خوێندووە.
