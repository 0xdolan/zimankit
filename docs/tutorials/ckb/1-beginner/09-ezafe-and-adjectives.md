# 9. Ezafe: linking nouns and adjectives

**Level:** 🟢 Beginner · lesson 9 of 23

**On this page:** [The linking vowel](#the-linking-vowel) · [Indefinite: a good man](#indefinite-a-good-man) · [Definite: the good man](#definite-the-good-man) · [Adjective as a statement](#adjective-as-a-statement) · [How zimankit reads these](#how-zimankit-reads-these) · [Practice](#practice) · [Summary](#summary)

## The linking vowel

When a noun is followed by something that describes it, such as an adjective, another noun or a pronoun, Sorani puts a small **linking vowel**, the **ezafe**, between them. In Sorani it is **-ی** (*-î*, after a vowel *-y*):

| phrase | parts | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| پیاوی باش | پیاو + ی + باش | a good man |
| ماڵی باوکم | ماڵ + ی + باوکم | my father's house |
| زمانی کوردی | زمان + ی + کوردی | the Kurdish language |
| دەرگای ماڵ | دەرگا + ی + ماڵ | the door of the house |
| کتێبی من | کتێب + ی + من | my book (= the book of me) |

Adjectives always come **after** the noun, linked by the ezafe.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| **پیاو** | man | **پیاو**<br><sub>pyaw</sub> | **پیاوی**<br><sub>pyawî</sub> | **پیاوێکی**<br><sub>pyawêkî</sub> |
| **کتێب** | book | **کتێب**<br><sub>kitêb</sub> | **کتێبی**<br><sub>kitêbî</sub> | **کتێبێکی**<br><sub>kitêbêkî</sub> |
| **دەرگا** | door | **دەرگا**<br><sub>derga</sub> | **دەرگای**<br><sub>dergay</sub> | **دەرگایەکی**<br><sub>dergayekî</sub> |
| **ماسی** | fish | **ماسی**<br><sub>masî</sub> | **ماسیی**<br><sub>masî</sub> | **ماسییەکی**<br><sub>masîyekî</sub> |
| **خانە** | house, room | **خانە**<br><sub>xane</sub> | **خانەی**<br><sub>xaney</sub> | **خانەیەکی**<br><sub>xaneyekî</sub> |
<!-- /gen -->

## Indefinite: a good man

With *a*, the indefinite ending comes first and the ezafe follows it: **پیاوێکی باش** *a good man*, **دەرگایەکی گەورە** *a big door*.

## Definite: the good man

With *the*, two things change:

1. the definite ending **moves to the end of the whole phrase**;
2. the ezafe after the noun becomes **-ە** (after a vowel **-یە**).

| | indefinite | definite |
|---|---|---|
| man, good | پیاوێکی باش *a good man* | **پیاوە باشەکە** *the good man* |
| men, good | پیاوانی باش *good men* | **پیاوە باشەکان** *the good men* |
| door, big | دەرگایەکی گەورە *a big door* | **دەرگا گەورەکە** *the big door* |
| book, new | کتێبێکی نوێ *a new book* | **کتێبە نوێیەکە** *the new book* |

In دەرگا گەورەکە the ezafe disappears after the vowel ا in everyday writing (*dergay gewreke* is also heard).

## Adjective as a statement

When the adjective **says something about** the noun (*the man is good*), there is no ezafe. The copula ending goes on the adjective instead: **پیاوەکە باشە** *the man is good* vs **پیاوە باشەکە** *the good man*.

## How zimankit reads these

<!-- gen:analyze words=کتێبی,پیاوێکی,پیاوەکە,کتێبەکان -->
| word | segmentation | morpheme tags | lemma | features |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **کتێبی** | کتێب+ی | STEM+3SG/EZ | کتێب | enclitic=3sg|ezafe |
| **پیاوێکی** | پیاو+ێک+ی | STEM+INDEF+3SG/EZ | پیاو | definite=ind, number=sg, enclitic=3sg|ezafe |
| **پیاوەکە** | پیاو+ەکە | STEM+DEF.SG | پیاو | definite=def, number=sg |
| **کتێبەکان** | کتێب+ەکان | STEM+DEF.PL | کتێب | definite=def, number=pl |
<!-- /gen -->

The final ی of کتێبی is analysed as `enclitic=3sg|ezafe`: in isolation it could be the ezafe or *his/her* (lesson 11). Context decides. That is why a grammar checker has to look at the next word.

## Practice

Translate:

1. *a big house* (گەورە *big*)
2. *the big house*
3. *the house is big*

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. ماڵێکی گەورە
2. ماڵە گەورەکە
3. ماڵەکە گەورەیە

</details>

## Summary

* Noun **-ی** + modifier: پیاوی باش.
* *a* + adjective: پیاوێکی باش.
* *the* + adjective: noun **-ە** adjective **-ەکە**: پیاوە باشەکە.
* Statement: پیاوەکە باشە (no ezafe).
