# 8. This, that and question words

**Level:** 🟢 Beginner · lesson 8 of 23

**On this page:** [This and that](#this-and-that) · [Question words](#question-words) · [Practice](#practice) · [Summary](#summary)

## This and that

Sorani frames the noun between a **demonstrative before it** and the ending **-ە** (after a vowel **-یە**) after it:

| | singular | plural |
|---|---|---|
| this | **ئەم** … **ە** | **ئەم** … **انە** |
| that | **ئەو** … **ە** | **ئەو** … **انە** |

<!-- gen:noun nouns=پیاو,کتێب,دەرگا,ماسی,خانە cells=bare,dem -->
| noun | meaning | bare (generic) | ئەم / ئەو … ە |
|---|---|---|---|
| **پیاو** | man | **پیاو**<br><sub>pyaw</sub> | **پیاوە**<br><sub>pyawe</sub> |
| **کتێب** | book | **کتێب**<br><sub>kitêb</sub> | **کتێبە**<br><sub>kitêbe</sub> |
| **دەرگا** | door | **دەرگا**<br><sub>derga</sub> | **دەرگایە**<br><sub>dergaye</sub> |
| **ماسی** | fish | **ماسی**<br><sub>masî</sub> | **ماسییە**<br><sub>masîye</sub> |
| **خانە** | house, room | **خانە**<br><sub>xane</sub> | **خانەیە**<br><sub>xaneye</sub> |
<!-- /gen -->

<!-- gen:sentences ipa=1
ئەم کتێبە باشە. | This book is good.
ئەو پیاوە مامۆستایە. | That man is a teacher.
ئەم پیاوانە کوردن. | These men are Kurds.
ئەو دەرگایە داخە. | Close that door.
-->
| # | Kurdish | Latin | IPA | English |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ئەم کتێبە باشە. | em kitêbe başe. | /ʔæm k(ɯ)teːbæ baːʃæ/ | This book is good. |
| 2 | ئەو پیاوە مامۆستایە. | ew pyawe mamostaye. | /ʔæw pjaːwæ maːmoːstaːjæ/ | That man is a teacher. |
| 3 | ئەم پیاوانە کوردن. | em pyawane kurdin. | /ʔæm pjaːwaːnæ kuɾd(ɯ)n/ | These men are Kurds. |
| 4 | ئەو دەرگایە داخە. | ew dergaye daxe. | /ʔæw dæɾɡaːjæ daːxæ/ | Close that door. |
<!-- /gen -->

Used **alone** (without a noun), *this* and *that* are pronouns:

| | singular | plural |
|---|---|---|
| this (one) | **ئەمە** | **ئەمانە** |
| that (one) | **ئەوە** | **ئەوانە** |

ئەمە چییە؟ *What is this?* — ئەوە کتێبە. *That is a book.*

## Question words

| word | Latin | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **کێ** | kê | who |
| **چی** | çî | what |
| **کام** | kam | which |
| **چۆن** | çon | how |
| **کەی** | key | when |
| **بۆچی** | boçî | why |
| **لەکوێ** | lekwê | where |
| **کوا** | kwa | where is …? |
| **چەند** | çend | how many, how much |

The question word usually stands **where the answer would be**, which is often just before the verb:

<!-- gen:sentences
ئەوە کێیە؟ | Who is that?
ئەمە چییە؟ | What is this?
ناوت چییە؟ | What is your name?
لەکوێ دەژیت؟ | Where do you live?
بۆچی هاتیت؟ | Why did you come?
چەند کتێبت هەیە؟ | How many books do you have?
-->
| # | Kurdish | Latin | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ئەوە کێیە؟ | ewe kêye? | Who is that? |
| 2 | ئەمە چییە؟ | eme çîye? | What is this? |
| 3 | ناوت چییە؟ | nawt çîye? | What is your name? |
| 4 | لەکوێ دەژیت؟ | lekwê dejît? | Where do you live? |
| 5 | بۆچی هاتیت؟ | boçî hatît? | Why did you come? |
| 6 | چەند کتێبت هەیە؟ | çend kitêbit heye? | How many books do you have? |
<!-- /gen -->

## Practice

1. Say *this woman* and *those women*.
2. Ask *Who is this man?*

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. ئەم ژنە — ئەو ژنانە
2. ئەم پیاوە کێیە؟

</details>

## Summary

* *this / that + noun*: ئەم / ئەو … ە (plural … انە).
* Alone: ئەمە، ئەوە، ئەمانە، ئەوانە.
* Question words go where the answer would be.
