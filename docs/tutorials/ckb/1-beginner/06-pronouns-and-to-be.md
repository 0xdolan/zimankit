# 6. Pronouns and “to be”

**Level:** 🟢 Beginner · lesson 6 of 23

**On this page:** [Personal pronouns](#personal-pronouns) · [“To be”: the copula endings](#to-be-the-copula-endings) · [“Is not”: نیم، نیت، نییە…](#is-not-نیم-نیت-نییە) · [“There is”: هەیە / نییە](#there-is-هەیە--نییە) · [Questions](#questions) · [Practice](#practice) · [Summary](#summary)

## Personal pronouns

| person | pronoun | Latin | meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1sg | **من** | min | I, me |
| 2sg | **تۆ** | to | you (one person) |
| 3sg | **ئەو** | ew | he, she, it; that |
| 1pl | **ئێمە** | ême | we, us |
| 2pl | **ئێوە** | êwe | you (several people, or polite) |
| 3pl | **ئەوان** | ewan | they, them |

There is **no gender**: ئەو means *he*, *she* and *it*. The same pronouns are used for subjects and objects (من *I* and *me*).

## “To be”: the copula endings

English *am / are / is* is not a separate word in Sorani. It is a small **ending** attached to the word that says what someone is, like a short form of *I'm*:

| person | after a consonant | after a vowel |
|---|---|---|
| 1sg | **-م** -im | **-م** -m |
| 2sg | **-یت** -ît | **-یت** -yt |
| 3sg | **-ە** -e | **-یە** -ye |
| 1pl | **-ین** -în | **-ین** -yn |
| 2pl | **-ن** -in | **-ن** -n |
| 3pl | **-ن** -in | **-ن** -n |

With کورد *Kurd* (consonant-final) and مامۆستا *teacher* (vowel-final):

<!-- gen:sentences ipa=1
من کوردم. | I am Kurdish.
تۆ کوردیت. | You are Kurdish.
ئەو کوردە. | He / She is Kurdish.
ئێمە کوردین. | We are Kurdish.
ئێوە کوردن. | You (pl) are Kurdish.
ئەوان کوردن. | They are Kurdish.
من مامۆستام. | I am a teacher.
ئەو مامۆستایە. | He / She is a teacher.
-->
| # | Kurdish | Latin | IPA | English |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | من کوردم. | min kurdim. | /m(ɯ)n kuɾd(ɯ)m/ | I am Kurdish. |
| 2 | تۆ کوردیت. | to kurdît. | /toː kuɾdiːt/ | You are Kurdish. |
| 3 | ئەو کوردە. | ew kurde. | /ʔæw kuɾdæ/ | He / She is Kurdish. |
| 4 | ئێمە کوردین. | ême kurdîn. | /ʔeːmæ kuɾdiːn/ | We are Kurdish. |
| 5 | ئێوە کوردن. | êwe kurdin. | /ʔeːwæ kuɾd(ɯ)n/ | You (pl) are Kurdish. |
| 6 | ئەوان کوردن. | ewan kurdin. | /ʔæwaːn kuɾd(ɯ)n/ | They are Kurdish. |
| 7 | من مامۆستام. | min mamostam. | /m(ɯ)n maːmoːstaːm/ | I am a teacher. |
| 8 | ئەو مامۆستایە. | ew mamostaye. | /ʔæw maːmoːstaːjæ/ | He / She is a teacher. |
<!-- /gen -->

Because the ending shows the person, the pronoun can be dropped: **کوردم** alone means *I am Kurdish*.

## “Is not”: نیم، نیت، نییە…

The negative copula is a separate word placed after the noun or adjective:

| person | form | example |
|---|---|---|
| 1sg | **نیم** | من مامۆستا نیم. *I am not a teacher.* |
| 2sg | **نیت** | تۆ ماندوو نیت. *You are not tired.* |
| 3sg | **نییە** | ئەو لێرە نییە. *He is not here.* |
| 1pl | **نین** | ئێمە کورد نین. *We are not Kurds.* |
| 2pl / 3pl | **نین** | ئەوان لێرە نین. *They are not here.* |

## “There is”: هەیە / نییە

**هەیە** means *there is / exists*; its negative is **نییە** *there isn't*. Combined with a personal clitic (lesson 11) it expresses *to have*:

<!-- gen:sentences
ئاو هەیە. | There is water.
نان نییە. | There is no bread.
کتێبێکم هەیە. | I have a book. (lit. a-book-my exists)
ئۆتۆمبێلت هەیە؟ | Do you have a car?
-->
| # | Kurdish | Latin | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ئاو هەیە. | aw heye. | There is water. |
| 2 | نان نییە. | nan nîye. | There is no bread. |
| 3 | کتێبێکم هەیە. | kitêbêkim heye. | I have a book. (lit. a-book-my exists) |
| 4 | ئۆتۆمبێلت هەیە؟ | otombêlt heye? | Do you have a car? |
<!-- /gen -->

## Questions

A yes/no question is a statement said with rising intonation and written with **؟**: ئەو مامۆستایە؟ *Is he a teacher?* You can also start it with **ئایا** (formal): ئایا ئەو مامۆستایە؟

## Practice

Make sentences with the copula:

1. *I am a student* (قوتابی *student*).
2. *We are teachers.*
3. *She is not here* (لێرە *here*).

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. من قوتابیم. (قوتابی ends in a vowel → -م)
2. ئێمە مامۆستاین.
3. ئەو لێرە نییە.

</details>

## Summary

* Six pronouns, no gender: من تۆ ئەو ئێمە ئێوە ئەوان.
* *To be* is an ending: -م -یت -ە/-یە -ین -ن -ن.
* *Not to be*: نیم نیت نییە نین; *there is*: هەیە.
