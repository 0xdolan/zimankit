# 10. The present tense

**Level:** 🟢 Beginner · lesson 10 of 23

**On this page:** [Two stems](#two-stems) · [Building the present](#building-the-present) · [Six verbs in the present](#six-verbs-in-the-present) · [The negative](#the-negative) · [Meaning of the present](#meaning-of-the-present) · [Word order: verb last](#word-order-verb-last) · [Practice](#practice) · [Summary](#summary)

## Two stems

Every Sorani verb has **two stems**, and you must learn both:

| infinitive | meaning | past stem | present stem |
|---|---|---|---|
| کردن | to do | کرد | **کە** |
| خواردن | to eat | خوارد | **خۆ** |
| نووسین | to write | نووسی | **نووس** |
| چوون | to go | چوو | **چ** |
| ڕۆیشتن | to go, to leave | ڕۆیشت | **ڕۆ** |
| زانین | to know | زانی | **زان** |

The **past stem** is the infinitive minus its final **ن**. The **present stem** is often quite different and must be learned with each verb: it is listed in every dictionary, and on every verb page of the [reference](../reference/verbs/README.md).

## Building the present

> **دە** + present stem + personal ending

The personal endings (called *set 1*) are almost the same as the copula of lesson 6:

| person | ending | after a vowel-final stem |
|---|---|---|
| 1sg | **-م** | -م |
| 2sg | **-یت** | -یت |
| 3sg | **-ێت** | **-ات** after ە, **-وات** after ۆ, **-ت** after ا / ێ |
| 1pl | **-ین** | -ین |
| 2pl | **-ن** | -ن |
| 3pl | **-ن** | -ن |

Colloquially the final ت of the 3sg is often dropped: دەکا, دەڵێ.

هاتن *to come* is irregular: present دێ-, negative نایە-.

## The negative

Replace **دە** with **نا**: دەچم *I go* → ناچم *I don't go*.

## Meaning of the present

The دە- present covers English *I do*, *I am doing* and the near future *I will do*:

<!-- gen:sentences ipa=1
من نان دەخۆم. | I eat / am eating bread.
ئەو نامە دەنووسێت. | She writes / is writing a letter.
ئێمە سبەی دەچین بۆ هەولێر. | We are going to Erbil tomorrow.
ئەوان کوردی نازانن. | They don't know Kurdish.
تۆ چی دەکەیت؟ | What are you doing?
-->
| # | Kurdish | Latin | IPA | English |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | من نان دەخۆم. | min nan dexom. | /m(ɯ)n naːn dæxoːm/ | I eat / am eating bread. |
| 2 | ئەو نامە دەنووسێت. | ew name denûsêt. | /ʔæw naːmæ dænuːseːt/ | She writes / is writing a letter. |
| 3 | ئێمە سبەی دەچین بۆ هەولێر. | ême sbey deçîn bo hewlêr. | /ʔeːmæ sbæj dætʃiːn boː hæwleːɾ/ | We are going to Erbil tomorrow. |
| 4 | ئەوان کوردی نازانن. | ewan kurdî nazanin. | /ʔæwaːn kuɾdiː naːzaːn(ɯ)n/ | They don't know Kurdish. |
| 5 | تۆ چی دەکەیت؟ | to çî dekeyt? | /toː tʃiː dækæjt/ | What are you doing? |

## Word order: verb last

Sorani is **SOV**: Subject, Object, then the Verb at the end. Time and place expressions usually come right after the subject:

> **من** (S) — **ئەمڕۆ** (when) — **لە ماڵ** (where) — **کتێب** (O) — **دەخوێنم** (V)
> *I am reading a book at home today.*

## Summary

* Present = **دە** + present stem + **م یت ێت ین ن ن**; negative with **نا**.
* Learn the present stem with every verb.
* The verb comes last.
