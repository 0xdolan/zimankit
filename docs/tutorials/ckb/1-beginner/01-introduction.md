## What is Sorani?

Kurdish is a West Iranian language, related to Persian, Balochi and more distantly to English. It is not one uniform language but a group of closely related varieties. The two with the most speakers are:

* **Central Kurdish**, usually called **Sorani** (*سۆرانی*), spoken by about 6–8 million people in the Kurdistan Region of Iraq (Erbil / *هەولێر*, Sulaymaniyah / *سلێمانی*, Kirkuk) and in western Iran (Sanandaj, Mahabad);
* **Northern Kurdish**, usually called **Kurmanji**, which has its own course in this series.

Sorani is an official language of Iraq. It is used in schools, universities, newspapers, television and government, which makes it one of the best-documented Kurdish varieties.

## The writing system in one minute

Sorani is written in an **Arabic-based alphabet from right to left**. It differs from the Arabic script in three ways that make it much easier for a learner:

1. **Most vowels are written.** Arabic leaves short vowels out; Sorani writes ا ە ێ ۆ و ی as full letters. Only one short vowel, a quick *i* sound, is left unwritten (lesson 3).
2. **It has letters Arabic lacks**: ڕ ڵ ێ ۆ پ چ ژ گ ڤ.
3. **There is one form per word.** You never need to guess vowels from context the way readers of Arabic or Persian do.

Every Sorani word in this course is also given in a Latin transliteration and in IPA, so you can start reading before you know the alphabet.

## What makes Sorani grammar interesting

You will meet these ideas step by step. Here is a preview so nothing comes as a surprise:

| idea | example | lesson |
|---|---|---|
| “the” is an **ending**, not a word | پیاو *a man* → پیاوەکە *the man* | 7 |
| little **personal endings** (clitics) do the work of *my, your, …* | کتێبەکەم *my book* | 11 |
| the verb comes **last** in the sentence | من نان دەخۆم *I bread eat* = I eat bread | 10 |
| in the past tense, **transitive** verbs mark the doer with a clitic instead of an ending (ergativity) | هاتم *I came* but کردم *I did* | 13–14 |
| there is **no grammatical gender** and no case endings on nouns | ئەو = he / she / it | 6 |

## How this course works

* Lessons 1–10 (🟢) teach reading, pronunciation and the core grammar of simple sentences.
* Lessons 11–17 (🟡) add possession, the past tense and longer sentences.
* Lessons 18–23 (🔴) complete the verb system and the written standard.

* Sorani is Central Kurdish, written right to left in an Arabic-based alphabet that writes almost all vowels.
