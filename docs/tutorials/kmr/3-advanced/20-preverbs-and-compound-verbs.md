# 20. Preverbs and compound verbs

**Level:** 🔴 Advanced · lesson 20 of 23

**On this page:** [Preverbs](#preverbs) · [Compound verbs](#compound-verbs) · [Summary](#summary)

## Preverbs

A **preverb** is a short element before the verb that changes its meaning: **ra-** *up*, **hil-** *up*, **ve-** *open, back*, **der-** *out*, **da-** *down*, **wer-** *towards*, **lê-**, **pê-**, **tê-**, **çê-**:

| verb | parts | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| rabûn | ra + bûn | to get up |
| hilgirtin | hil + girtin | to pick up, to lift |
| vekirin | ve + kirin | to open |
| derketin | der + ketin | to go out |
| vexwarin | ve + xwarin | to drink |
| çêkirin | çê + kirin | to make, to repair |

Rules:

* The present prefix comes **after** the preverb: ra**di**bim *I get up*, hil**di**girim, ve**di**kim.
* The subjunctive and imperative **drop bi-**: *rabim* “that I get up”, *rabe!* “get up!”, *veke!* “open!”
* Transitivity comes from the base verb, so past tenses follow it: *ez rabûm* (intransitive), *min derî vekir* (transitive, ergative).

## Compound verbs

Very many verbs are **noun or adjective + light verb** (usually *kirin*, *bûn*, *dan*, *anîn*), written as **separate words**:

| verb | literally | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| dest pê kirin | hand at do | to begin |
| bi kar anîn | with work bring | to use |
| alîkarî kirin | help do | to help |
| telefon kirin | phone do | to phone |
| nas kirin | known do | to know (a person) |
| bawer kirin | belief do | to believe |

Only the light verb is conjugated; the other part stays in front of it: *ez dest pê dikim* “I begin”, *min dest pê kir* “I began”, *ez wî nas dikim* “I know him”.

## Summary

* Preverb + di-: radibim; subjunctive and imperative without bi-: rabe, veke.
* Compound verbs: noun + light verb written apart; only the light verb is conjugated.
* Transitivity (and so ergativity) comes from the light verb.
