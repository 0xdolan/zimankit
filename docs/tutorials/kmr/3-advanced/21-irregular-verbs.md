# 21. Irregular and special verbs

**Level:** 🔴 Advanced · lesson 21 of 23

**On this page:** [hatin *to come*](#hatin-to-come) · [anîn *to bring*](#anîn-to-bring) · [çûn *to go*](#çûn-to-go) · [zanîn *to know* and karîn *can*](#zanîn-to-know-and-karîn-can) · [bûn *to be, to become*](#bûn-to-be-to-become) · [Unpredictable present stems](#unpredictable-present-stems) · [Summary](#summary)

Kurmanji endings are regular. What you have to memorise is the **present stem** of each verb and a few contractions.

## hatin *to come*

Present stem **ê**, which contracts with the prefixes: **têm** (di + ê), **nayêm**, **bêm**, **neyêm**; imperative **were / werin**, prohibitive **neyê / neyên**.

<!-- gen:verb verb=hatin cells=present,present_neg,subj,imperative -->
*hatin* (هاتن) “to come” — present stem **ê**, past stem **hat**, intransitive

| person | pronoun | Present | Present negative | Subjunctive | Imperative |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1sg | ez | **têm**<br><sub>تێم</sub> | **nayêm**<br><sub>نایێم</sub> | **bêm**<br><sub>بێم</sub> | — |
| 2sg | tu | **têyî**<br><sub>تێیی</sub> | **nayêyî**<br><sub>نایێیی</sub> | **bêyî**<br><sub>بێیی</sub> | **were**<br><sub>وەرە</sub> |
| 3sg | ew | **tê**<br><sub>تێ</sub> | **nayê**<br><sub>نایێ</sub> | **bê**<br><sub>بێ</sub> | — |
| 1pl | em | **tên**<br><sub>تێن</sub> | **nayên**<br><sub>نایێن</sub> | **bên**<br><sub>بێن</sub> | — |
| 2pl | hûn | **tên**<br><sub>تێن</sub> | **nayên**<br><sub>نایێن</sub> | **bên**<br><sub>بێن</sub> | **werin**<br><sub>وەرن</sub> |
| 3pl | ew | **tên**<br><sub>تێن</sub> | **nayên**<br><sub>نایێن</sub> | **bên**<br><sub>بێن</sub> | — |

## anîn *to bring*

Present stem **în**: **tînim** *I bring*, **nayînim**, **bînim**.

<!-- gen:verb verb=anîn cells=present,present_neg,subj,past -->
*anîn* (ئانین) “to bring” — present stem **în**, past stem **anî**, transitive

| person | agent (oblique) | object (direct) | Present | Present negative | Subjunctive | Simple past |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1sg | min | ez | **tînim**<br><sub>تینم</sub> | **nayînim**<br><sub>نایینم</sub> | **bînim**<br><sub>بینم</sub> | **anîm**<br><sub>ئانیم</sub> |
| 2sg | te | tu | **tînî**<br><sub>تینی</sub> | **nayînî**<br><sub>نایینی</sub> | **bînî**<br><sub>بینی</sub> | **aniyî**<br><sub>ئانیی</sub> |
| 3sg | wî / wê | ew | **tîne**<br><sub>تینە</sub> | **nayîne**<br><sub>نایینە</sub> | **bîne**<br><sub>بینە</sub> | **anî**<br><sub>ئانی</sub> |
| 1pl | me | em | **tînin**<br><sub>تینن</sub> | **nayînin**<br><sub>نایینن</sub> | **bînin**<br><sub>بینن</sub> | **anîn**<br><sub>ئانین</sub> |
| 2pl | we | hûn | **tînin**<br><sub>تینن</sub> | **nayînin**<br><sub>نایینن</sub> | **bînin**<br><sub>بینن</sub> | **anîn**<br><sub>ئانین</sub> |
| 3pl | wan | ew | **tînin**<br><sub>تینن</sub> | **nayînin**<br><sub>نایینن</sub> | **bînin**<br><sub>بینن</sub> | **anîn**<br><sub>ئانین</sub> |

> Past tenses of a transitive verb are **ergative**: the agent is oblique and the verb agrees with the **object** (the *object* column), e.g. *min tu dîtî* “I saw you”.

## zanîn *to know* and karîn *can*

Negated with **ni-** instead of *na-*: **nizanim** *I don't know*, **nikarim** *I can't*.

*zanîn* (زانین) “to know” — present stem **zan**, past stem **zanî**, transitive

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1sg | min | ez | **dizanim**<br><sub>دزانم</sub> | **nizanim**<br><sub>نزانم</sub> | **zanîm**<br><sub>زانیم</sub> |
| 2sg | te | tu | **dizanî**<br><sub>دزانی</sub> | **nizanî**<br><sub>نزانی</sub> | **zaniyî**<br><sub>زانیی</sub> |
| 3sg | wî / wê | ew | **dizane**<br><sub>دزانە</sub> | **nizane**<br><sub>نزانە</sub> | **zanî**<br><sub>زانی</sub> |

## bûn *to be, to become*

*bûn* is *to become* and supplies the subjunctive and past of *to be*: **dibim** *I become*, **bibim** *(that) I be*, **bûm** *I was / became*. The present *am / is / are* is the copula of lesson 6.

<!-- gen:verb verb=bûn cells=present,subj,past,perfect -->
*bûn* (بوون) “to be, to become” — present stem **b**, past stem **bû**, intransitive

| person | pronoun | Present | Subjunctive | Simple past | Present perfect |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1sg | ez | **dibim**<br><sub>دبم</sub> | **bibim**<br><sub>ببم</sub> | **bûm**<br><sub>بووم</sub> | **bûme**<br><sub>بوومە</sub> |
| 2sg | tu | **dibî**<br><sub>دبی</sub> | **bibî**<br><sub>ببی</sub> | **bûyî**<br><sub>بوویی</sub> | **bûyî**<br><sub>بوویی</sub> |
| 3sg | ew | **dibe**<br><sub>دبە</sub> | **bibe**<br><sub>ببە</sub> | **bû**<br><sub>بوو</sub> | **bûye**<br><sub>بوویە</sub> |
| 1pl | em | **dibin**<br><sub>دبن</sub> | **bibin**<br><sub>ببن</sub> | **bûn**<br><sub>بوون</sub> | **bûne**<br><sub>بوونە</sub> |
| 2pl | hûn | **dibin**<br><sub>دبن</sub> | **bibin**<br><sub>ببن</sub> | **bûn**<br><sub>بوون</sub> | **bûne**<br><sub>بوونە</sub> |
| 3pl | ew | **dibin**<br><sub>دبن</sub> | **bibin**<br><sub>ببن</sub> | **bûn**<br><sub>بوون</sub> | **bûne**<br><sub>بوونە</sub> |
<!-- /gen -->

*To have* in the past and subjunctive uses **hebûn**: *pirtûkeke min hebû* “I had a book”.

## Unpredictable present stems

These are the stems to memorise. The endings are regular:

| infinitive | present stem | 1sg present |
|---|---|---|
| kirin *do* | k | dikim |
| xwarin *eat* | xw | dixwim |
| dîtin *see* | bîn | dibînim |
| gotin *say* | bêj | dibêjim |
| dan *give* | d | didim |
| birin *carry* | b | dibim |
| xwestin *want* | xwaz | dixwazim |
| man *stay* | mîn | dimînim |
| kuştin *kill* | kuj | dikujim |
| şûştin *wash* | şo | dişom |
| hiştin *leave* | hêl | dihêlim |
| bihîstin *hear* | bihîz | dibihîzim |
| firotin *sell* | firoş | difiroşim |

Notice that *dibim* is both *I become* (bûn) and *I carry* (birin). Context decides; the analyzer returns both readings.

## Summary

* hatin: têm / nayêm / bêm / were; anîn: tînim.
* çûn: here! — zanîn, karîn: ni-.
* bûn: become / was; hebûn: had.
* Learn the present stem of every verb.
