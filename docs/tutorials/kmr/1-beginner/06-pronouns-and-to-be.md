# 6. Pronouns and “to be”

**Level:** 🟢 Beginner · lesson 6 of 23

**On this page:** [Two sets of pronouns](#two-sets-of-pronouns) · [“To be”: the copula](#to-be-the-copula) · [“Not to be”](#not-to-be) · [“There is” and “to have”: heye / tune](#there-is-and-to-have-heye--tune) · [Questions](#questions) · [Practice](#practice) · [Summary](#summary)

## Two sets of pronouns

Kurmanji pronouns have two forms. The **direct** form is used for the subject of most sentences. The **oblique** form is used after prepositions, for objects of present-tense verbs, for owners (*my, your*) and for the doer in the past tense (lesson 14).

| person | direct | oblique | meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1sg | **ez** | **min** | I / me, my |
| 2sg | **tu** | **te** | you / you, your |
| 3sg (that) | **ew** | **wî** (m) / **wê** (f) | he, she, it / him, her, his, her |
| 3sg (this) | **ev** | **vî** (m) / **vê** (f) | this one |
| 1pl | **em** | **me** | we / us, our |
| 2pl | **hûn** | **we** | you (pl) / you, your |
| 3pl (those) | **ew** | **wan** | they / them, their |
| 3pl (these) | **ev** | **van** | these |

Gender appears only in the oblique 3sg: *wî* for a man (or masculine noun), *wê* for a woman (or feminine noun).

## “To be”: the copula

*Am, are, is* is a short **word written after** the noun or adjective:

| person | after a consonant | after a vowel |
|---|---|---|
| 1sg | **im** | **me** |
| 2sg | **î** | **yî** |
| 3sg | **e** | **ye** |
| 1pl / 2pl / 3pl | **in** | **ne** |

<!-- gen:sentences ipa=1
Ez kurd im. | I am Kurdish.
Tu kurd î. | You are Kurdish.
Ew kurd e. | He / She is Kurdish.
Em kurd in. | We are Kurdish.
Ez mamoste me. | I am a teacher.
Tu mamoste yî. | You are a teacher.
Ew mamoste ye. | He / She is a teacher.
Hûn mamoste ne. | You (pl) are teachers.
-->
| # | Kurdish | Arabic script | IPA | English |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ez kurd im. | ئەز کورد ئم. | /ɛz kʊɾd ɪm/ | I am Kurdish. |
| 2 | Tu kurd î. | تو کورد ئی. | /tʊ kʊɾd iː/ | You are Kurdish. |
| 3 | Ew kurd e. | ئەو کورد ئە. | /ɛw kʊɾd ɛ/ | He / She is Kurdish. |
| 4 | Em kurd in. | ئەم کورد ئن. | /ɛm kʊɾd ɪn/ | We are Kurdish. |
| 5 | Ez mamoste me. | ئەز مامۆستە مە. | /ɛz maːmoːstɛ mɛ/ | I am a teacher. |
| 6 | Tu mamoste yî. | تو مامۆستە یی. | /tʊ maːmoːstɛ jiː/ | You are a teacher. |
| 7 | Ew mamoste ye. | ئەو مامۆستە یە. | /ɛw maːmoːstɛ jɛ/ | He / She is a teacher. |
| 8 | Hûn mamoste ne. | هوون مامۆستە نە. | /huːn maːmoːstɛ nɛ/ | You (pl) are teachers. |
<!-- /gen -->

The plural has one form for all persons: *em / hûn / ew … in*.

## “Not to be”

Put **ne** before the noun or adjective; the copula stays at the end:

<!-- gen:sentences
Ez ne mamoste me. | I am not a teacher.
Ew ne li malê ye. | He is not at home.
-->
| # | Kurdish | Arabic script | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ez ne mamoste me. | ئەز نە مامۆستە مە. | I am not a teacher. |
| 2 | Ew ne li malê ye. | ئەو نە ل مالێ یە. | He is not at home. |
<!-- /gen -->

## “There is” and “to have”: heye / tune

**heye** *there is* (plural **hene**), negative **tune** or **tuneye**. With an owner in the oblique (or an ezafe phrase) it means *to have*:

<!-- gen:sentences ipa=1
Av heye. | There is water.
Nan tune. | There is no bread.
Pirtûkeke min heye. | I have a book.
Du birayên min hene. | I have two brothers.
-->
| # | Kurdish | Arabic script | IPA | English |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Av heye. | ئاڤ هەیە. | /aːv hɛjɛ/ | There is water. |
| 2 | Nan tune. | نان تونە. | /naːn tʊnɛ/ | There is no bread. |
| 3 | Pirtûkeke min heye. | پرتووکەکە من هەیە. | /pɪɾtuːkɛkɛ mɪn hɛjɛ/ | I have a book. |
| 4 | Du birayên min hene. | دو برایێن من هەنە. | /dʊ bɪɾaːjeːn mɪn hɛnɛ/ | I have two brothers. |
<!-- /gen -->

## Questions

A yes/no question can be a statement with a rising tone, or start with the particle **ma**: *Ma tu kurd î?* “Are you Kurdish?”

## Practice

1. *I am a student* (*xwendekar*).
2. *We are not teachers.*
3. *She is beautiful* (*ciwan*).

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. Ez xwendekar im.
2. Em ne mamoste ne.
3. Ew ciwan e.

</details>

## Summary

* Direct: ez tu ew em hûn ew; oblique: min te wî/wê me we wan.
* Copula after the word: im î e in (after vowels me yî ye ne).
* *Not*: ne … ; *there is*: heye/hene, negative tune.
