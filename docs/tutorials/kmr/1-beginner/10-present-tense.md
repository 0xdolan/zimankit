# 10. The present tense

**Level:** 🟢 Beginner · lesson 10 of 23

**On this page:** [Two stems](#two-stems) · [Building the present](#building-the-present) · [Special prefixes](#special-prefixes) · [Word order and the object](#word-order-and-the-object) · [Practice](#practice) · [Summary](#summary)

## Two stems

Every Kurmanji verb has a **past stem** and a **present stem**:

| infinitive | meaning | past stem | present stem |
|---|---|---|---|
| kirin | to do | kir | **k** |
| xwarin | to eat | xwar | **xw** |
| dîtin | to see | dît | **bîn** |
| çûn | to go | çû | **ç** |
| gotin | to say | got | **bêj** |
| xwendin | to read | xwend | **xwîn** |

The **past stem** is the infinitive minus **-in** (or **-n** after a vowel). The **present stem** is often unpredictable and must be learned with each verb; the [verb reference](../reference/verbs/README.md) lists it for every verb in the course.

## Building the present

> **di-** + present stem + personal ending

| person | after a consonant | after a vowel |
|---|---|---|
| 1sg | **-im** | **-m** |
| 2sg | **-î** | **-yî** |
| 3sg | **-e** | — |
| pl (we, you, they) | **-in** | **-n** |

The negative replaces *di-* with **na-**.

## Special prefixes

* A present stem beginning with **ê** or **î** contracts *di-* to **t-** and *na-* to **nay-**: *hatin* → **têm** “I come”, **nayêm**; *anîn* → **tînim** “I bring”, **nayînim**.
* A stem beginning with **a** keeps *di-* and merges *na-*: *axaftin* → **diaxivim** “I speak”, **naxivim**.
* *zanîn* “to know” and *karîn* “can” are negated with **ni-**: **nizanim** “I don't know”.

## Word order and the object

The verb comes **last** (SOV). A definite object of a present-tense verb is **oblique** (lesson 9):

<!-- gen:sentences ipa=1
Ez nan dixwim. | I eat / am eating bread.
Ez pirtûkê dixwînim. | I am reading the book.
Tu çi dikî? | What are you doing?
Ew kurdî nizane. | He doesn't know Kurdish.
Em sibê diçin Amedê. | We are going to Amed tomorrow.
Ez te dibînim. | I see you.
-->
| # | Kurdish | Arabic script | IPA | English |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ez nan dixwim. | ئەز نان دخوم. | /ɛz naːn dɪxʷɯm/ | I eat / am eating bread. |
| 2 | Ez pirtûkê dixwînim. | ئەز پرتووکێ دخوینم. | /ɛz pɪɾtuːkeː dɪxʷiːnɯm/ | I am reading the book. |
| 3 | Tu çi dikî? | تو چ دکی؟ | /tʊ tʃɪ dɪkiː/ | What are you doing? |
| 4 | Ew kurdî nizane. | ئەو کوردی نزانە. | /ɛw kʊɾdiː nɪzaːnɛ/ | He doesn't know Kurdish. |
| 5 | Em sibê diçin Amedê. | ئەم سبێ دچن ئامەدێ. | /ɛm sɪbeː dɪtʃɯn aːmɛdeː/ | We are going to Amed tomorrow. |
| 6 | Ez te dibînim. | ئەز تە دبینم. | /ɛz tɛ dɪbiːnɯm/ | I see you. |
<!-- /gen -->

The present covers *I do*, *I am doing* and often *I will do*.

## Summary

* Present = **di-** + present stem + **-im -î -e -in**; negative **na-**.
* ê/î-stems: **t-**, **nay-**; zanîn/karîn: **ni-**.
* The verb is last; a definite object is oblique.
