# Northern Kurdish (Kurmanji) course

**Kurmancî** is the most widely spoken Kurdish variety, used in Turkey, Syria, the Duhok area of Iraq, parts of Iran and Armenia, and the diaspora. It is written in the Latin-based Hawar alphabet. This course takes you from your first letter to split ergativity and the full verb system.

## How to study

1. Read a lesson from top to bottom and **say every example aloud**. Use the IPA column if you are unsure.
2. Learn every noun **with its gender** (*jin f*, *bav m*), because Kurmanji endings depend on it.
3. Do the practice section before you open the answers.
4. Move on when the summary at the end of the lesson feels obvious.
