# 12. Wishes, commands and the future

**Level:** 🟡 Intermediate · lesson 12 of 23

**On this page:** [The subjunctive](#the-subjunctive) · [When to use it](#when-to-use-it) · [The future](#the-future) · [The imperative](#the-imperative) · [Practice](#practice) · [Summary](#summary)

## The subjunctive

The subjunctive is the form for wanted, possible or not-yet-real actions. It is the present with **bi-** instead of *di-*; the negative uses **ne-**:

<!-- gen:verb verb=kirin cells=present,subj,subj_neg -->
*kirin* (کرن) “to do, to make” — present stem **k**, past stem **kir**, transitive

| person | pronoun | Present | Subjunctive | Subjunctive negative |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1sg | ez | **dikim**<br><sub>دکم</sub> | **bikim**<br><sub>بکم</sub> | **nekim**<br><sub>نەکم</sub> |
| 2sg | tu | **dikî**<br><sub>دکی</sub> | **bikî**<br><sub>بکی</sub> | **nekî**<br><sub>نەکی</sub> |
| 3sg | ew | **dike**<br><sub>دکە</sub> | **bike**<br><sub>بکە</sub> | **neke**<br><sub>نەکە</sub> |
| 1pl | em | **dikin**<br><sub>دکن</sub> | **bikin**<br><sub>بکن</sub> | **nekin**<br><sub>نەکن</sub> |
| 2pl | hûn | **dikin**<br><sub>دکن</sub> | **bikin**<br><sub>بکن</sub> | **nekin**<br><sub>نەکن</sub> |
| 3pl | ew | **dikin**<br><sub>دکن</sub> | **bikin**<br><sub>بکن</sub> | **nekin**<br><sub>نەکن</sub> |
<!-- /gen -->

<!-- gen:verb verb=çûn cells=subj,subj_neg -->
*çûn* (چوون) “to go” — present stem **ç**, past stem **çû**, intransitive

| person | pronoun | Subjunctive | Subjunctive negative |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1sg | ez | **biçim**<br><sub>بچم</sub> | **neçim**<br><sub>نەچم</sub> |
| 2sg | tu | **biçî**<br><sub>بچی</sub> | **neçî**<br><sub>نەچی</sub> |
| 3sg | ew | **biçe**<br><sub>بچە</sub> | **neçe**<br><sub>نەچە</sub> |
| 1pl | em | **biçin**<br><sub>بچن</sub> | **neçin**<br><sub>نەچن</sub> |
| 2pl | hûn | **biçin**<br><sub>بچن</sub> | **neçin**<br><sub>نەچن</sub> |
| 3pl | ew | **biçin**<br><sub>بچن</sub> | **neçin**<br><sub>نەچن</sub> |
<!-- /gen -->

ê/î-stems contract: *hatin* → **bêm** “that I come”, *anîn* → **bînim**.

## When to use it

| after | meaning | example |
|---|---|---|
| **dixwazim** | I want (to) | Ez dixwazim biçim. *I want to go.* |
| **dikarim** | I can | Ez dikarim bêm. *I can come.* |
| **divê** | must | Divê tu bixwînî. *You must study.* |
| **bila** | let | Bila ew bê. *Let him come.* |
| **heke / eger** | if | Heke tu bêyî, em ê biçin. *If you come, we will go.* |
| **da ku** | in order to | Ez hatim da ku te bibînim. *I came to see you.* |

<!-- gen:verb verb=xwestin cells=present -->
*xwestin* (خوەستن) “to want” — present stem **xwaz**, past stem **xwest**, transitive

| person | pronoun | Present |
|---|---|---|
| 1sg | ez | **dixwazim**<br><sub>دخوازم</sub> |
| 2sg | tu | **dixwazî**<br><sub>دخوازی</sub> |
| 3sg | ew | **dixwaze**<br><sub>دخوازە</sub> |
| 1pl | em | **dixwazin**<br><sub>دخوازن</sub> |
| 2pl | hûn | **dixwazin**<br><sub>دخوازن</sub> |
| 3pl | ew | **dixwazin**<br><sub>دخوازن</sub> |
<!-- /gen -->

## The future

The future is the subjunctive preceded by the particle **dê** or its short form **ê**, placed after the subject. It is very often written joined to a pronoun:

| person | future of *çûn* |
|---|---|
| 1sg | ez ê biçim / **ezê** biçim |
| 2sg | tu yê biçî / **tuyê** biçî |
| 3sg | ew ê biçe / **ewê** biçe |
| pl | em ê / hûn ê / ew ê biçin |

<!-- gen:sentences ipa=1
Ez ê sibê biçim Wanê. | I will go to Van tomorrow.
Tu yê çi bikî? | What will you do?
Ew ê neyê. | He won't come.
Em ê bibînin. | We will see.
-->
| # | Kurdish | Arabic script | IPA | English |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ez ê sibê biçim Wanê. | ئەز ئێ سبێ بچم وانێ. | /ɛz eː sɪbeː bɪtʃɯm waːneː/ | I will go to Van tomorrow. |
| 2 | Tu yê çi bikî? | تو یێ چ بکی؟ | /tʊ jeː tʃɪ bɪkiː/ | What will you do? |
| 3 | Ew ê neyê. | ئەو ئێ نەیێ. | /ɛw eː nɛjeː/ | He won't come. |
| 4 | Em ê bibînin. | ئەم ئێ ببینن. | /ɛm eː bɪbiːnɯn/ | We will see. |
<!-- /gen -->

The negative future puts *ne-* on the verb: *ez ê neçim* “I won't go”.

## The imperative

Commands are **bi-** + present stem + **-e** (singular) or **-in** (plural); after a vowel-final stem, nothing / **-n**. The prohibitive (*don't!*) uses **ne-**:

<!-- gen:verb verb=kirin cells=imperative,prohibitive -->
*kirin* (کرن) “to do, to make” — present stem **k**, past stem **kir**, transitive

| person | pronoun | Imperative | Prohibitive |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2sg | tu | **bike**<br><sub>بکە</sub> | **neke**<br><sub>نەکە</sub> |
| 2pl | hûn | **bikin**<br><sub>بکن</sub> | **nekin**<br><sub>نەکن</sub> |
<!-- /gen -->

<!-- gen:verb verb=xwarin cells=imperative,prohibitive -->
*xwarin* (خوارن) “to eat” — present stem **xw**, past stem **xwar**, transitive

| person | pronoun | Imperative | Prohibitive |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2sg | tu | **bixwe**<br><sub>بخوە</sub> | **nexwe**<br><sub>نەخوە</sub> |
| 2pl | hûn | **bixwin**<br><sub>بخون</sub> | **nexwin**<br><sub>نەخون</sub> |
<!-- /gen -->

<!-- gen:verb verb=xwendin cells=imperative,prohibitive -->
*xwendin* (خوەندن) “to read, to study” — present stem **xwîn**, past stem **xwend**, transitive

| person | pronoun | Imperative | Prohibitive |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2sg | tu | **bixwîne**<br><sub>بخوینە</sub> | **nexwîne**<br><sub>نەخوینە</sub> |
| 2pl | hûn | **bixwînin**<br><sub>بخوینن</sub> | **nexwînin**<br><sub>نەخوینن</sub> |
<!-- /gen -->

Two very common verbs have irregular imperatives:

<!-- gen:verb verb=hatin cells=imperative,prohibitive -->
*hatin* (هاتن) “to come” — present stem **ê**, past stem **hat**, intransitive

| person | pronoun | Imperative | Prohibitive |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2sg | tu | **were**<br><sub>وەرە</sub> | **neyê**<br><sub>نەیێ</sub> |
| 2pl | hûn | **werin**<br><sub>وەرن</sub> | **neyên**<br><sub>نەیێن</sub> |
<!-- /gen -->

<!-- gen:verb verb=çûn cells=imperative,prohibitive -->
*çûn* (چوون) “to go” — present stem **ç**, past stem **çû**, intransitive

| person | pronoun | Imperative | Prohibitive |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2sg | tu | **here**<br><sub>هەرە</sub> | **neçe**<br><sub>نەچە</sub> |
| 2pl | hûn | **herin**<br><sub>هەرن</sub> | **neçin**<br><sub>نەچن</sub> |
<!-- /gen -->

## Practice

1. *I want to eat.*
2. *We will come tomorrow.*
3. *Don't go!* (to one person)

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. Ez dixwazim bixwim.
2. Em ê sibê bên.
3. Neçe!

</details>

## Summary

* Subjunctive **bi-** (negative **ne-**) after *want, can, must, let, if, in order to*.
* Future: **dê / ê** + subjunctive (ezê biçim).
* Imperative **bike / bikin**, prohibitive **neke**; *were* come!, *here* go!
