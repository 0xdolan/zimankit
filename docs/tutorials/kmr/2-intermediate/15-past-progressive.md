# 15. Was doing, used to do: the past progressive

**Level:** 🟡 Intermediate · lesson 15 of 23

**On this page:** [Form](#form) · [Meaning](#meaning) · [All past forms side by side](#all-past-forms-side-by-side) · [Practice](#practice) · [Summary](#summary)

## Form

> **di-** + past stem + ending; negative **nedi-**

The ergativity rules of lesson 14 apply exactly as in the simple past: with a transitive verb the agent is oblique and the verb agrees with the object.

<!-- gen:verb verb=hatin cells=past_prog,past_prog_neg -->
*hatin* (هاتن) “to come” — present stem **ê**, past stem **hat**, intransitive

| person | pronoun | Past progressive | Past progressive negative |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1sg | ez | **dihatim**<br><sub>دهاتم</sub> | **nedihatim**<br><sub>نەدهاتم</sub> |
| 2sg | tu | **dihatî**<br><sub>دهاتی</sub> | **nedihatî**<br><sub>نەدهاتی</sub> |
| 3sg | ew | **dihat**<br><sub>دهات</sub> | **nedihat**<br><sub>نەدهات</sub> |
| 1pl | em | **dihatin**<br><sub>دهاتن</sub> | **nedihatin**<br><sub>نەدهاتن</sub> |
| 2pl | hûn | **dihatin**<br><sub>دهاتن</sub> | **nedihatin**<br><sub>نەدهاتن</sub> |
| 3pl | ew | **dihatin**<br><sub>دهاتن</sub> | **nedihatin**<br><sub>نەدهاتن</sub> |
<!-- /gen -->

<!-- gen:verb verb=xwarin cells=past_prog,past_prog_neg -->
*xwarin* (خوارن) “to eat” — present stem **xw**, past stem **xwar**, transitive

| person | agent (oblique) | object (direct) | Past progressive | Past progressive negative |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1sg | min | ez | **dixwarim**<br><sub>دخوارم</sub> | **nedixwarim**<br><sub>نەدخوارم</sub> |
| 2sg | te | tu | **dixwarî**<br><sub>دخواری</sub> | **nedixwarî**<br><sub>نەدخواری</sub> |
| 3sg | wî / wê | ew | **dixwar**<br><sub>دخوار</sub> | **nedixwar**<br><sub>نەدخوار</sub> |
| 1pl | me | em | **dixwarin**<br><sub>دخوارن</sub> | **nedixwarin**<br><sub>نەدخوارن</sub> |
| 2pl | we | hûn | **dixwarin**<br><sub>دخوارن</sub> | **nedixwarin**<br><sub>نەدخوارن</sub> |
| 3pl | wan | ew | **dixwarin**<br><sub>دخوارن</sub> | **nedixwarin**<br><sub>نەدخوارن</sub> |

> Past tenses of a transitive verb are **ergative**: the agent is oblique and the verb agrees with the **object** (the *object* column), e.g. *min tu dîtî* “I saw you”.
<!-- /gen -->

## Meaning

1. an action **in progress** in the past: *I was eating when he came*;
2. a past **habit**: *I used to read every evening*;
3. *would* in unreal conditions.

Ez diçûm dibistanê. | I was going / used to go to school.

## Practice

1. *I was coming.*
2. *She used to write letters* (*name*).
3. *We weren't going.*

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. Ez dihatim.
2. Wê name dinivîsîn. (plural object → plural verb; with one letter: name dinivîsî)
3. Em nediçûn.

</details>

## Summary

* Past progressive = **di-** + past stem; negative **nedi-**.
* Ergative with transitive verbs, like every past tense.
