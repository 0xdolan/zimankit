# 11. This, that and question words

**Level:** 🟡 Intermediate · lesson 11 of 23

**On this page:** [Demonstratives](#demonstratives) · [Question words](#question-words) · [Practice](#practice) · [Summary](#summary)

## Demonstratives

*This* is **ev**, *that* is **ew**. Like pronouns, they have direct and oblique forms, and in the oblique the noun takes its oblique ending too:

| | direct | oblique feminine | oblique masculine | oblique plural |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| this | **ev** jin | **vê** jinê | **vî** mirovî | **van** jinan |
| that | **ew** jin | **wê** jinê | **wî** mirovî | **wan** jinan |

<!-- gen:sentences ipa=1
Ev pirtûk nû ye. | This book is new.
Ew mirov mamoste ye. | That man is a teacher.
Ez vê pirtûkê dixwînim. | I am reading this book.
Ez wî mirovî nas dikim. | I know that man.
Van rojan pir germ e. | These days it is very hot.
-->
| # | Kurdish | Arabic script | IPA | English |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ev pirtûk nû ye. | ئەڤ پرتووک نوو یە. | /ɛv pɪɾtuːk nuː jɛ/ | This book is new. |
| 2 | Ew mirov mamoste ye. | ئەو مرۆڤ مامۆستە یە. | /ɛw mɪɾoːv maːmoːstɛ jɛ/ | That man is a teacher. |
| 3 | Ez vê pirtûkê dixwînim. | ئەز ڤێ پرتووکێ دخوینم. | /ɛz veː pɪɾtuːkeː dɪxʷiːnɯm/ | I am reading this book. |
| 4 | Ez wî mirovî nas dikim. | ئەز وی مرۆڤی ناس دکم. | /ɛz wiː mɪɾoːviː naːs dɪkɯm/ | I know that man. |
| 5 | Van rojan pir germ e. | ڤان ڕۆژان پر گەرم ئە. | /vaːn roːʒaːn pɪɾ ɡɛɾm ɛ/ | These days it is very hot. |
<!-- /gen -->

The masculine oblique ending **-î**, often dropped elsewhere, is normal after *vî / wî*: *vî bajarî* “this city”.

Alone, **ev** and **ew** mean *this one*, *that one*, and also *he / she / it / they*: *Ev çi ye?* “What is this?”

## Question words

| word | meaning |
|---|---|
| **kî** | who |
| **çi** | what |
| **kîjan** | which |
| **çawa** | how |
| **çima** | why |
| **kengî** | when |
| **li ku** / **ku derê** | where |
| **ji ku** | from where |
| **çend** | how many, how much |
| **ma** | yes/no question particle |

The question word stands where the answer would be, usually before the verb:

<!-- gen:sentences
Ev kî ye? | Who is this?
Navê te çi ye? | What is your name?
Tu li ku dijî? | Where do you live?
Tu ji ku yî? | Where are you from?
Çima tu nayêyî? | Why don't you come?
Çend pirtûkên te hene? | How many books do you have?
-->
| # | Kurdish | Arabic script | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ev kî ye? | ئەڤ کی یە؟ | Who is this? |
| 2 | Navê te çi ye? | ناڤێ تە چ یە؟ | What is your name? |
| 3 | Tu li ku dijî? | تو ل کو دژی؟ | Where do you live? |
| 4 | Tu ji ku yî? | تو ژ کو یی؟ | Where are you from? |
| 5 | Çima tu nayêyî? | چما تو نایێیی؟ | Why don't you come? |
| 6 | Çend pirtûkên te hene? | چەند پرتووکێن تە هەنە؟ | How many books do you have? |
<!-- /gen -->

## Practice

1. *this house* (*mal f*), direct and oblique.
2. *I see those men* (*mirov*, present *dibînim*).

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. ev mal — vê malê
2. Ez wan mirovan dibînim.

</details>

## Summary

* ev / ew (direct); vê, vî, van / wê, wî, wan (oblique) + oblique ending on the noun.
* Question words: kî çi kîjan çawa çima kengî li ku çend; particle ma.
