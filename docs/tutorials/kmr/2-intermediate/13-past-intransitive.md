# 13. The past tense I: intransitive verbs

**Level:** 🟡 Intermediate · lesson 13 of 23

**On this page:** [Transitive or intransitive?](#transitive-or-intransitive) · [The simple past](#the-simple-past) · [In sentences](#in-sentences) · [Practice](#practice) · [Summary](#summary)

## Transitive or intransitive?

From now on you must know whether a verb is **transitive** (it takes a direct object: *eat something*, *see someone*) or **intransitive** (it does not: *come, go, fall, sleep*). Kurmanji builds the past of the two kinds in completely different ways. This lesson covers the easy kind, intransitive verbs. The [verb reference](../reference/verbs/README.md) marks every verb.

## The simple past

> **past stem** + personal ending (the subject is in the **direct** case)

| person | after a consonant | after a vowel |
|---|---|---|
| 1sg | **-im** | **-m** |
| 2sg | **-î** | **-yî** |
| 3sg | — | — |
| pl | **-in** | **-n** |

<!-- gen:verb verb=hatin cells=past,past_neg -->
*hatin* (هاتن) “to come” — present stem **ê**, past stem **hat**, intransitive

| person | pronoun | Simple past | Past negative |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1sg | ez | **hatim**<br><sub>هاتم</sub> | **nehatim**<br><sub>نەهاتم</sub> |
| 2sg | tu | **hatî**<br><sub>هاتی</sub> | **nehatî**<br><sub>نەهاتی</sub> |
| 3sg | ew | **hat**<br><sub>هات</sub> | **nehat**<br><sub>نەهات</sub> |
| 1pl | em | **hatin**<br><sub>هاتن</sub> | **nehatin**<br><sub>نەهاتن</sub> |
| 2pl | hûn | **hatin**<br><sub>هاتن</sub> | **nehatin**<br><sub>نەهاتن</sub> |

<!-- gen:verb verb=man cells=past,past_neg -->
*man* (مان) “to stay, to remain” — present stem **mîn**, past stem **ma**, intransitive

| person | pronoun | Simple past | Past negative |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1sg | ez | **mam**<br><sub>مام</sub> | **nemam**<br><sub>نەمام</sub> |
| 2sg | tu | **mayî**<br><sub>مایی</sub> | **nemayî**<br><sub>نەمایی</sub> |
| 3sg | ew | **ma**<br><sub>ما</sub> | **nema**<br><sub>نەما</sub> |
| 1pl | em | **man**<br><sub>مان</sub> | **neman**<br><sub>نەمان</sub> |
| 2pl | hûn | **man**<br><sub>مان</sub> | **neman**<br><sub>نەمان</sub> |
| 3pl | ew | **man**<br><sub>مان</sub> | **neman**<br><sub>نەمان</sub> |
<!-- /gen -->

The negative is **ne-**: *nehatim* “I didn't come”.

## In sentences

<!-- gen:sentences ipa=1
Ez duh hatim. | I came yesterday.
Ew çûn bazarê. | They went to the market.
Zarok ket. | The child fell.
Tu çima nehatî? | Why didn't you come?
Em li malê man. | We stayed at home.
-->
| # | Kurdish | Arabic script | IPA | English |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ez duh hatim. | ئەز دوه هاتم. | /ɛz dʊh haːtɯm/ | I came yesterday. |
| 2 | Ew çûn bazarê. | ئەو چوون بازارێ. | /ɛw tʃuːn baːzaːɾeː/ | They went to the market. |
| 3 | Zarok ket. | زارۆک کەت. | /zaːɾoːk kɛt/ | The child fell. |
| 4 | Tu çima nehatî? | تو چما نەهاتی؟ | /tʊ tʃɪmaː nɛhaːtiː/ | Why didn't you come? |
| 5 | Em li malê man. | ئەم ل مالێ مان. | /ɛm lɪ maːleː maːn/ | We stayed at home. |
<!-- /gen -->

The subject is direct (*ez, tu, ew*) and the verb agrees with it, just as in English. In the next lesson this changes.

## Practice

Put into the past: *ez diçim*, *ew tê*, *em dimînin*.

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

*ez çûm*, *ew hat*, *em man*.

</details>

## Summary

* Intransitive past = past stem + **-im -î — -in**; negative **ne-**.
* Subject in the direct case; the verb agrees with it.
